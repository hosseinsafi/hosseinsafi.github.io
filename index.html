01 Profile

A consultant who bridges deep technical analysis and the strategic decisions leaders actually have to make.

I'm a Senior Analyst at Real Wireless, working at the intersection of wireless innovation, non terrestrial networks, and AI driven communication systems. My focus is helping clients translate emerging technology into clear, defensible strategy across future network architectures, hybrid RF optical systems, and data driven performance evaluation.

Before joining Real Wireless, I was a Research Associate at the University of Cambridge, contributing to flagship UK programmes on next generation connectivity. I hold a PhD in Telecommunications Engineering and am a UK Global Talent awardee.

I work best where rigour matters: investment cases, regulatory submissions, market models, and board level recommendations that need to hold up under scrutiny.
